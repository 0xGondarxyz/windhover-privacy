# Windhover Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 28 September 2026

Windhover is a speed-reading app for iPhone and iPad, made by Murat Can Yuksel, an individual developer ("I", "me").

**The short version:** your books, reading positions and settings never leave your device. The only data that goes to a server is the record of your Windhover Pro purchase, which is anonymous. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Who is responsible for your data

Murat Can Yuksel is the data controller for the processing described here.
Contact: [muratcanyukselpro@gmail.com](mailto:muratcanyukselpro@gmail.com)

## Data that stays on your device

- **Your books.** Files you import (EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3 or TXT), the text taken from them, your library, bookmarks of where you stopped, and your settings are stored only inside the app on your device. They are never uploaded, and I never see them.
- **File access.** You choose files through Apple's system document picker. The app can read only the files you pick.
- **No account.** There is no sign-up and no login.

Deleting a book removes it from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Data that leaves your device: your Pro purchase

When you buy or restore Windhover Pro:

- **Apple** handles the payment. I never receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies to that.
- **RevenueCat, Inc.** (United States) checks with Apple whether you own Pro, so the app can unlock it and you can restore it on another device. RevenueCat receives:
  - a random anonymous ID that the app creates (not your name, email or Apple Account),
  - your purchase history for this app: which product, when, the price and currency, and the transaction details Apple provides,
  - the technical details needed to make that check, such as the app version, the operating system version and your store country. Like any internet connection, the connection also reveals your IP address to RevenueCat's servers.

I use this data to unlock and restore Pro, to prevent fraudulent purchases, and to see total sales figures in RevenueCat's dashboard. It is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising or tracking, and not sold.

**Legal basis (EU and UK GDPR):** performance of the contract you entered by buying Pro (Article 6(1)(b)), and my legitimate interest in preventing fraud and understanding total sales (Article 6(1)(f)).

**Transfers outside the EU:** RevenueCat stores this data on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States. It processes the data only on my behalf, under its [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.revenuecat.com/dpa), which uses the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers. See also [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## Emails you send me

If you write to me, for example with "Send feedback" in Settings, I receive your email address and your message. I use them only to answer you (legal basis: my legitimate interest in replying to your request, Article 6(1)(f)), and I delete them within 12 months of our last exchange.

## What the app does not do

Windhover has no ads, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no tracking across apps or websites, so it never shows Apple's tracking prompt. It does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

## How long data is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete the book or the app.
- **Purchase records at RevenueCat:** as long as needed to let you restore Pro and to meet tax and legal duties. If you ask me to delete your record, I will; a later "Restore purchases" simply creates a new anonymous record from Apple's receipt.
- **Emails:** deleted within 12 months of our last exchange.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me to show you, correct, delete or export the data I hold about you, or to limit or stop using it. If you are in the EU, the EEA or the UK, these are your rights under the GDPR, and you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email [muratcanyukselpro@gmail.com](mailto:muratcanyukselpro@gmail.com). Because purchase records are anonymous, please include the order ID from Apple's receipt email so I can find yours. I will answer within one month.

## Children

Windhover is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data beyond the anonymous purchase record described above, from anyone.
