## Common questions

**How do I add a book?**
In the library, tap **Add books** and pick a file from the Files app, iCloud Drive or another storage location. Windhover reads EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3 and TXT files.

**Why won't a book open?**
- Books with copy protection (DRM), such as most books bought from Kindle or Apple Books, cannot be opened. Use a DRM-free copy.
- Scanned PDFs contain pictures of pages, not text, so there is nothing to read.

**How do I get Pro on a new iPhone or iPad?**
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Settings in Windhover and tap **Restore purchases**. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

**How do I get a refund?**
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Where are my books stored?**
Only on your device, inside the app. They are not uploaded or synced. Deleting the app deletes them, so keep your original files.
